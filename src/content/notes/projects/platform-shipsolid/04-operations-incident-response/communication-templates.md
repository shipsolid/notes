## Communication Templates

## Purpose

Copy-paste communication templates for incidents.

## Initial notification

```text
[SEV-n] <short title> — investigating
Impact: <who/what>
Started: <time>
IC: <name>
Next update: <time>
```

## Update

```text
[SEV-n] <title> — update
Status: <investigating | identified | monitoring>
What we know: <...>
Next update: <time>
```

## Resolution

```text
[SEV-n] <title> — resolved
Duration: <start>–<end>
Impact: <summary>
Post-mortem: <link>
```
