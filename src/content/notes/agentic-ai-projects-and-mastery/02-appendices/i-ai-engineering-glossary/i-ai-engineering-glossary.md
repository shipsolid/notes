## Purpose

A quick-reference glossary for terms used throughout the book, meant for lookup rather than
sequential reading. Entries below are seeded from terminology that's genuinely ambiguous or easy to
misuse in conversation; more entries accrue here as later chapters introduce terms worth a
standalone definition.

| Term                              | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Agentic engineer (ambiguous term) | Has two legitimate, unrelated meanings — always disambiguate which one is meant: (1) someone who **uses agents to engineer**, i.e. a software engineer highly skilled at using coding agents like Claude Code or Codex as a tool; (2) someone who **engineers agents**, i.e. an AI engineer who builds agentic systems. The bare phrase alone is genuinely ambiguous, not just informal — spell out which meaning is intended when precision matters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Believable vs. correct            | An LLM is trained to produce plausible, believable output — that is literally its training objective. Nothing about that training guarantees the output is accurate or useful. Fluency and confidence are not evidence of correctness; the only way to tell the difference is to measure against a real, checkable outcome. See [[ai-foundations/01-language-models-in-practice/08-hallucination-management/08-hallucination-management\|Hallucination Management]] for the mechanisms behind why this happens, and [[agentic-ai-engineering/00-introduction-to-agentic-ai/06-agent-design-principles/06-agent-design-principles\|Agent Design Principles]] for the design discipline this distinction motivates. |
