## Parts

### 00 — Foundations

Before drilling any section, the reader needs to know what these tests actually gate, how they're
scored, and how to practice without wasting reps. This Part sets up vocabulary and strategy the
other four Parts assume.

- [[01-what-aptitude-tests-assess|1 — What Aptitude Tests Actually Assess]] — _(stub)_ — why
  aptitude rounds still exist in MAANG-adjacent pipelines (NQT, off-campus OAs, vendor-run screens)
  even when the core loop is DSA/system-design
- [[02-test-format-and-scoring|2 — Test Format & Scoring]] — _(stub)_ — sectional cutoffs, negative
  marking math, adaptive vs. fixed-form tests
- [[03-study-and-practice-strategy|3 — Study & Practice Strategy]] — _(stub)_ — spaced practice,
  keeping an error log, the speed-vs-accuracy tradeoff

- [[01-number-systems-hcf-lcm|1 — Number Systems, HCF & LCM]] — _(stub)_ — divisibility rules,
  remainders, factors, LCM/HCF shortcuts
- [[02-percentages-profit-loss-interest|2 — Percentages, Profit-Loss & Interest]] — _(stub)_ —
  percentage change chains, discount/markup, simple vs. compound interest
- [[03-ratios-averages-mixtures|3 — Ratios, Averages & Mixtures]] — _(stub)_ — ratio-proportion,
  weighted averages, alligation/mixture problems
- [[04-time-speed-distance-and-work|4 — Time-Speed-Distance & Time-Work]] — _(stub)_ — relative

- [[05-permutations-combinations-probability|5 — Permutations, Combinations & Probability]] —

- [[06-data-interpretation|6 — Data Interpretation]] — _(stub)_ — tables, bar/line/pie charts,

Pattern recognition and structured deduction — the section that most reliably separates prepared

curriculum.

- [[01-blood-relations-direction-sense-coding-decoding|1 — Blood Relations, Direction Sense & Coding-Decoding]]
  — _(stub)_ — family-tree notation, compass-direction tracking, letter/number coding schemes
- [[02-syllogisms-and-statement-based-reasoning|2 — Syllogisms & Statement-Based Reasoning]] —
  _(stub)_ — Venn-diagram method for syllogisms, statement-conclusion, statement-assumption
- [[03-series-and-analogies|3 — Series & Analogies]] — _(stub)_ — number/letter series patterns,

- [[04-puzzles-and-seating-arrangement|4 — Puzzles & Seating Arrangement]] — _(stub)_ —
  linear/circular arrangement, grid puzzles, data-sufficiency framing
