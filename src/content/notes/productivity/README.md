## Suggested Reading Order

For someone targeting **Principal/Staff Engineer (L6/L7)** roles, this sequence builds a durable
productivity system:

1. **Parts 00–02**: Foundations, self-management, and goal setting.
2. **Parts 03–04**: Time management and deep work.
3. **Parts 05–06**: Personal knowledge management and accelerated learning.
4. **Part 11**: Engineering productivity practices.
5. **Part 08**: Decision-making frameworks.
6. **Part 15**: Designing a personal operating system that scales with senior leadership
   responsibilities.

This progression aligns productivity with technical depth, strategic thinking, and long-term career
growth rather than focusing solely on completing more tasks.
