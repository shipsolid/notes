## 4. Cognitive Distortion Check

Check any that apply to the automatic thought above — most distorted thoughts involve more than one.

- [ ] **All-or-nothing thinking** — seeing things in black-and-white categories
- [ ] **Overgeneralization** — one bad event means it always happens
- [ ] **Mental filter** — dwelling on a single negative detail, ignoring the positives
- [ ] **Disqualifying the positive** — positive experiences "don't count"
- [ ] **Jumping to conclusions — mind reading** — assuming what others think without evidence
- [ ] **Jumping to conclusions — fortune telling** — predicting things will turn out badly
- [ ] **Magnification / catastrophizing** — blowing things out of proportion
- [ ] **Emotional reasoning** — "I feel it, so it must be true"
- [ ] **Should statements** — criticizing self/others with "should," "must," "ought to"
- [ ] **Labeling** — attaching a global negative label instead of describing the specific behavior
- [ ] **Personalization** — blaming self for something not entirely in my control
