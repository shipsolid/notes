## Emotional Processing Journal — Template

> Use this **as needed**, when an emotion is strong enough that it's hard to think clearly or move
> on. This is slower and more somatic than the [[01-1-daily-reflection-template]] — the goal is to
> fully feel and name the emotion, not just log that it happened.

## 1. Name the Emotion

Be as precise as possible — "frustrated" is not the same as "disrespected," "anxious" is not the
same as "overwhelmed." Naming an emotion precisely (affect labeling) reduces its intensity.

## 5. The Need Underneath

Emotions often signal an unmet need (safety, respect, autonomy, connection, competence, rest).

- **What need is this emotion pointing to?** [FILL]
- **Is there a way to meet that need directly right now?** [FILL]

## 6. Self-Compassion Check

- **What would I say to a friend feeling exactly this?** [FILL]
- **Can I offer myself that same response?** [FILL]
