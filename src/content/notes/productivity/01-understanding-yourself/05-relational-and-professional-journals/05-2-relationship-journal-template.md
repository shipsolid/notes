## 6. Gottman's Four Horsemen Check

Check any that showed up in the conversation — on either side.

- [ ] **Criticism** — attacking character rather than addressing the specific behavior
- [ ] **Contempt** — sarcasm, eye-rolling, mockery, or a sense of superiority
- [ ] **Defensiveness** — deflecting blame instead of hearing the concern
- [ ] **Stonewalling** — shutting down or withdrawing instead of engaging

## 7. Repair Action
